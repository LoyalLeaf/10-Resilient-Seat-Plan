    How seats are chosen
    • The six front desks are for eyesight difficulty. Fewest recitations sit farthest left.
    • Add an Eyesight column and mark Yes or No. Once anyone is marked, that column replaces the built-in six.
    • Everyone else zigzags from the bottom-left: left to right, then right to left, up to the back. Fewest recitations first. Ties break by last name. A leading de or de la is skipped.
    • Exact counts stay hidden until the class password. This page rechecks the spreadsheet on its own.
