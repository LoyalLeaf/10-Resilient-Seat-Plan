    How seats are chosen
    • Chahna Yap stays at the front-left eyesight seat, no matter her score. The other five eyesight seats rank left to right, fewest recitations first.
    • Everyone else zigzags from the front-left regular seats: left to right, then right to left, toward the back. Fewest first. The same score sorts by last name, skipping a leading de or de la.
    • Colors mark under 10, then 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, and 50. Exact counts stay hidden until the class password.
    • The pencil asks for that student’s own last four LRN digits, then saves the new count for the whole class.
